隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日 · 适用于 iPhone 版袋记（Swipr）1.0 及以后版本

一句话：开发者不收集你的任何数据。

袋记不需要注册账号，没有开发者服务器，没有广告、第三方统计或追踪。你的账单只保存在你的 iPhone 上，只有在你自己设置备份时，才会加密后存到你选择的位置。

1. 保存在你 iPhone 上的数据

为了记账，袋记会在本机保存以下内容：

  • 交易记录：金额、日期、商户、分类、备注、收支类型等；
  • 你导入的截图、CSV 和 PDF 账单，以及识别出的文字。确认入账后，原始截图默认会被清理；只有你开启「保留原始截图」后才会长期保存，你可以随时删除；
  • 分类、商户规则和应用设置。

截图和账单的识别全部在本机完成，使用 Apple 的 Vision 文字识别和随 App 安装的一个小型识别模型。袋记不会把任何图片、文字或账单内容发送给开发者或其他任何人。识别模型用合成数据训练，你的截图不会被用来训练模型。

本机数据库使用 iOS 的数据保护（Data Protection）存储，恢复密钥和 WebDAV 密码保存在系统钥匙串（Keychain）中。

2. 袋记会访问什么

  • 照片：通过系统的照片选择器导入，袋记只能拿到你选中的图片，看不到你的相册。
  • 分享和文件：只处理你从其他 App 分享过来、或在「文件」里选中的截图、CSV 和 PDF。
  • 快捷指令：你运行截图快捷指令时，指令把当时的屏幕截图交给袋记，在本机识别。
  • 面容 ID / 触控 ID：仅用于你开启的应用锁。验证由 iOS 完成，袋记拿不到你的面部或指纹信息。
  • 小组件和实时活动：会在主屏幕或锁定屏幕上显示识别结果和统计金额。你可以在系统设置中关闭它们。

袋记不使用定位、通讯录、麦克风或相机，不申请通知权限，也不访问你的广告标识符。

3. 袋记什么时候联网

记账和识别都不需要网络。袋记只会在以下由你主动发起的情况下联网：

  • WebDAV 备份：你填写自己的 WebDAV 服务器地址后，备份会先在本机用 AES-256-GCM 加密，再通过 HTTPS 上传到这台服务器。只有你的恢复密钥能解开备份，开发者无法访问你的服务器，也无法解开备份。
  • 购买袋记 Pro：付款、退款和恢复购买都由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。袋记只从 App Store 获知你是否已解锁 Pro，拿不到你的付款信息。
  • 从云盘打开文件：如果你在「文件」里选择 iCloud 云盘或其他云盘中的文件，下载由该云盘服务完成，适用它们自己的隐私政策。

4. 备份

  • 袋记自己的备份（存到本机或 WebDAV）都经过加密，只能用你的恢复密钥恢复。请自己妥善保存恢复密钥：开发者没有副本，丢失后无法找回。
  • 系统备份：如果你开启了 iPhone 的 iCloud 备份，或用电脑备份 iPhone，iOS 会把袋记的账本作为 App 数据一起备份。这一过程由 Apple 完成，不经过开发者。临时文件和缓存不会进入系统备份。

5. 崩溃报告

袋记不写运行日志，也没有集成任何崩溃收集 SDK。如果你在 iPhone「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进」中选择了「与 App 开发者共享」，Apple 可能会把 App 崩溃时的技术报告（不包含你的账单内容）提供给开发者，仅用于修复问题。你可以随时关闭这项共享。

6. 删除你的数据

  • 在袋记的「设置 › 隐私与安全 › 清空全部本地数据」中可以删除本机的全部账目、规则和原始截图。存在本机的加密备份会保留，方便你之后恢复；删除 App 会把它们和其他本机数据一起清除。
  • 你导出或备份到其他位置的文件（包括 WebDAV 服务器上的备份）由你自己管理，需要你在那里删除。

7. 儿童

袋记不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意获取任何人的个人信息。

8. 本政策的变更

如果袋记处理数据的方式发生变化，我们会先更新本页面和生效日期，重大变化还会在 App 更新说明中写明。

9. 联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，请发邮件至 ruezacct@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Applies to Swipr (袋记) for iPhone, version 1.0 and later

In short: the developer collects no data from you.

Swipr needs no account and has no developer server, ads, third-party analytics or tracking. Your records stay on your iPhone. They leave it only when you set up a backup yourself, and then they are encrypted first and stored where you choose.

1. Data kept on your iPhone

To keep your ledger, Swipr stores on the device:

  • Transactions: amount, date, merchant, category, note, type and similar fields.
  • Screenshots, CSV and PDF statements you import, and the text recognized in them. Screenshots are removed once their records are filed, unless you turn on “Keep original screenshots”; you can delete kept originals at any time.
  • Categories, merchant rules and app settings.

Recognition happens entirely on the device, using Apple's Vision text recognition and a small model that ships inside the app. Swipr never sends your images, text or records to the developer or anyone else. The model is trained on synthetic data; your screenshots are never used to train it.

The ledger is stored with iOS Data Protection. Your recovery key and WebDAV password are stored in the system Keychain.

2. What Swipr accesses

  • Photos: imports go through the system photo picker, so Swipr only receives the images you pick and cannot see your library.
  • Sharing and Files: Swipr only reads the screenshots, CSV and PDF files you share with it or pick in Files.
  • Shortcuts: when you run the capture shortcut, it hands the current screenshot to Swipr, which reads it on the device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used only for the app lock you turn on. iOS performs the check; Swipr never receives face or fingerprint data.
  • Widgets and Live Activities: show recognized results and totals on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You can turn them off in Settings.

Swipr does not use location, contacts, the microphone or the camera, does not ask for notification permission, and does not access your advertising identifier.

3. When Swipr goes online

Recording and recognition work offline. Swipr connects to the network only when you start one of these:

  • WebDAV backup: after you enter your own WebDAV server, each backup is encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM and uploaded to that server over HTTPS. Only your recovery key can open it; the developer has no access to your server and cannot decrypt your backups.
  • Buying Swipr Pro: payment, refunds and restoring purchases are handled by Apple's App Store. Swipr only learns from the App Store whether Pro is unlocked, never your payment details.
  • Opening files from cloud storage: if you pick a file stored in iCloud Drive or another provider in Files, that provider downloads it under its own privacy policy.

4. Backups

  • Swipr's own backups (on the device or on WebDAV) are encrypted and can only be restored with your recovery key. Keep it safe: the developer has no copy and cannot recover it for you.
  • System backups: if you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS includes Swipr's ledger with the app's data. Apple performs that backup; it does not pass through the developer. Temporary files and caches are left out of system backups.

5. Crash reports

Swipr writes no logs and contains no crash-reporting SDK. If you chose “Share with App Developers” under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share technical crash reports with the developer, which do not contain your records, to help fix problems. You can turn this off at any time.

6. Deleting your data

  • Settings › Privacy & security › Erase all local data in Swipr removes every entry, rule and kept screenshot on the device. Encrypted backups stored on the device stay so you can restore later; deleting the app removes them along with everything else.
  • Files you exported or backed up elsewhere, including backups on your WebDAV server, are yours to manage and delete there.

7. Children

Swipr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly obtain personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way Swipr handles data changes, this page and its effective date will be updated first, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
