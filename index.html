袋记支持

袋记是一款本地记账 App：用快捷指令截取付款页面，或导入截图、CSV、PDF 账单，在 iPhone 上识别交易，滑动卡片确认入账。不用注册，账单不上传。

常见问题

识别出的金额或商户不对？

识别结果不会自动入账。在卡片上点「补充」或编辑即可改正，之后同一商户会按你的选择分类。

换了新 iPhone，怎么把账本带过去？

在旧手机上做一次备份（存到本机后传过去，或存到你的 WebDAV 服务器），然后在新手机上用同一个恢复密钥恢复。用 iCloud 或电脑整机迁移 iPhone 时，账本也会随 App 数据一起迁移。

恢复密钥丢了怎么办？

恢复密钥只保存在你的 iPhone 钥匙串里和你自己记下的地方，开发者没有副本，无法帮你找回或解开旧备份。只要旧 iPhone 还能打开袋记，就可以在那里查看恢复密钥，或者换一个新的密钥后重新备份。

买过 Pro，重装后怎么恢复？

在 Pro 页面点「恢复购买」，并确认登录的是购买时使用的 Apple 账户。恢复购买需要联网。

如何退款？

退款由 Apple 处理，请前往 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。

如何删除我的数据？

在「设置 › 清空全部本地数据」中删除，或直接删除 App。备份到其他位置的文件需要你在那里自行删除。详见隐私政策。

Swipr Support

Swipr is a local expense tracker: capture a payment screen with a Shortcut, or import screenshots, CSV or PDF statements; Swipr reads the transactions on your iPhone and you file them with a swipe. No account, no uploads.

Questions

The amount or merchant was read wrong?

Nothing is filed without you. Tap Fix or edit the card to correct it; the merchant then keeps the category you chose.

How do I move my ledger to a new iPhone?

Back up on the old iPhone (to the device and carry the file over, or to your WebDAV server), then restore on the new one with the same recovery key. Moving your whole iPhone with iCloud or a computer also brings the ledger along with the app's data.

I lost my recovery key.

The recovery key lives only in your iPhone's Keychain and wherever you wrote it down. The developer has no copy and cannot recover it or open old backups. If Swipr still opens on your old iPhone, you can view the key there, or change to a new key and back up again.

How do I get Pro back after reinstalling?

Tap Restore purchase on the Pro page while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Restoring needs a connection.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings › Erase all local data, or delete the app. Backups you saved elsewhere are yours to delete there. See the Privacy Policy.
